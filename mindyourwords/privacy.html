Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Mind Your Words app on iOS and Android, made by Endri Labs (Erdi Haruni).

The short version

Mind Your Words does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app works fully offline and never connects to the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app remembers two settings on your device only: whether sounds are on and whether you use the light or dark theme. Team names, player names and avatars you type exist only while a game is open and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Sharing a result

If you tap Share at the end of a game, the app makes a picture of the result and hands it to your phone's own share menu. You choose where it goes (for example a messaging app). The app itself does not upload it.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. The app does not ask for access to your camera, photos, contacts, location or microphone.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the app is safe to use for players of all ages.

Changes

If a future version starts to collect any data (for example optional accounts), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the app's store listing will say so.
