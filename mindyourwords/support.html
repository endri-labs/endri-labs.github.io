How to play

  1. Split into 2–4 teams. One player describes, the rest of the team guesses.
  2. Describe the big word on the card, but never say the forbidden words under it.
  3. Correct +1 · Busted (said a forbidden word) −1 · Pass skips the word.
  4. When time runs out, the next team plays. The first team to reach the target score wins.

Common questions

Does it need internet? No, everything works offline.

The phone doesn't vibrate. Check that system haptics / vibration is turned on in your phone's settings.

No sound. Tap the speaker button at the top of the screen and check the phone isn't on silent.
